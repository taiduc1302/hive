# Output pattern

| Use case | Primary | Effort | Execution | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Most recent work | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| Faster/cheaper | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| Escalation | ... | ... | ... | ... |

Then state **Default now**, **Switch up when**, **Switch down when**, **Confidence**, and **Freshness**. Avoid a long catalog unless explicitly requested.

When personal feedback materially changes a ranking, also state:

- the task category the evidence applies to;
- whether the change comes from outcome/retry evidence, paired same-task cost/latency evidence, or both;
- the eligible sample/comparable-task count;
- the applied empirical score delta when the Advisor exposes it;
- any empirical cap that changed the raw delta before ranking.

Do not summarize a personalized recommendation as merely “this worked better for you” when the evidence audit or score breakdown is available. The recommendation should be traceable from final choice to score delta to evidence bucket.
